WHY PALSTER EXISTS

You're not the same person to everyone. Your feed shouldn't force you to be.

To your mother, you're her child. To your professor, a student. To your closest friend, someone completely different again. Most social apps flatten all of that into one profile and one follower count, hoping the right people happen to see it.

Palster works differently. Your branch, your year, your hostel block — the real communities you're already living inside — see you instantly, without a follow request or an audience built from zero. Pin the people who matter most. Post anonymously to the people who'd actually recognize your name, not the internet.

What's in Palster

Communities

Your branch, year, hostel, and interests — join instantly, seen by everyone who's already part of that world.

Groups

Build a group for your specific people. One shared thread, one place, no scattered chats.

Anonymous, for real

Not hidden from strangers online — hidden from the people who'd actually know it was you. Zero identity leak, moderated and protected.

Ping

Send someone a genuinely personal question, anonymously, and get a real reply back. Not a DM thread. One question, one honest answer.

RealMoji

React with your actual face, not a recycled emoji everyone else is using too.

Moments

Build shared photo memories with your people — first trips, first hangouts, the stuff worth keeping.

RVCE ONLY

Built for one campus, on purpose.

Palster isn't a public network. Every account is verified with an RVCE college email at signup — nobody outside campus gets in, and nothing you post here ever leaves it.

NO SCOREBOARD FOR STRANGERS

Standing that stays inside your world.

Streaks and standing are visible — but only to your own community, not the whole internet. It's people who actually know you noticing you show up, not strangers watching a number.
