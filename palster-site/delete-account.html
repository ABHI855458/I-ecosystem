Delete your account

Palster · Last updated: September 20, 2026

You can permanently delete your Palster account and its data at any time, from inside the app. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

How to delete your account

Account deletion is handled entirely in the app — there is no web form to fill in, and you do not need to contact us first.

  1. Open Palster and sign in, if you are not already.
  2. Go to your Profile tab.
  3. Tap the settings icon in the top-right corner.
  4. Choose Delete Account at the bottom of the menu.
  5. Confirm when prompted. Your account and data are removed immediately.

Can't access the app? If you have lost access to your device or can no longer sign in, email abisheksdpatel@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject line "Delete My Account". Requests are processed within 7 business days.

What is permanently deleted

When you delete your account, the following are erased:

  • Your pings and ping replies — everything you sent and received.
  • Your push notification device tokens — notifications stop immediately.
  • The photos and media you uploaded — removed from our storage.
  • Your personal details — email address, name, username, anonymous persona name, date of birth, and profile and banner photos are erased or replaced with non-identifying placeholders.
  • Your ability to sign in — the account is marked deleted and cannot be used again.

What may be retained, and why

A small amount of information can outlive the account. We would rather state this plainly than imply a cleaner erasure than actually happens:

  • Content in shared spaces. Posts you contributed to a group album or other shared record may remain visible to the other people in it, where removing it would destroy someone else's record of a shared moment. It is no longer attributed to your identity.
  • Previously reported content. If content of yours was reported, it may be retained in a form accessible to moderators so that the report can still be reviewed.
  • Legal and safety records. Information may be retained where required for legal, safety, or fraud-prevention purposes.

Full detail is in section 7 of the Privacy Policy.

How long it takes

Deleting from inside the app takes effect immediately — there is no waiting period and no grace period during which the account can be recovered. Email requests are processed within 7 business days of receipt.

Contact

Questions about deletion, or trouble completing it: abisheksdpatel@gmail.com
