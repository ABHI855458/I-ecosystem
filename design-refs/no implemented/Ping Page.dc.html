DESIGN NOTES

Hold, not tap. Two seconds of deliberate pressure is the whole thesis — the ring fills, the blur lifts continuously, and nothing opens by accident while scrolling.

Time is soft. "5h left" with a slow-glowing dot, never a ticking clock. Pressure comes from care, not from a timer.

Hierarchy by opacity, not color. To Reply is the brightest glass; Sent is nearly flat; Replies sit between. Cyan / violet / magenta appear only where something is live.

Reply stays open. Sending doesn't close the card — a second thought is a first-class action, so the camera resets in place and sent replies stack beneath it.

Nothing vanishes. Viewed replies persist in a quiet state; only the Ping Back affordance is temporary (24h), so the loop invites but never nags.

OPEN QUESTIONS
